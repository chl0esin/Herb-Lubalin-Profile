Who Was
Herb Lubalin

Image 1918Born in New York City. Trained at Cooper Union.
Image 1945Joins Sudler & Hennessy, rises to art director within a decade.
Image 1961Designs the "Mother & Child" logotype — a full sentence built from one letterform.

Image 1970Co-founds International Typeface Corporation (ITC) and launches Avant Garde magazine.

03 · Typefaces

The Type
He Left Behind

L&C Hairline — 1966
Drawn with Tom Carnase, years before ITC existed. Strokes pared down to the thinnest possible line, so light the letters nearly disappear until they're set at real size.
Ha Li
Avant Garde Gothic — 1970
Geometric, circular, built for the magazine masthead. Its tight ligatures became a design cliché the moment they became a design language.
Aa Ga
ITC Ronda — 1970
Angular and faceted, built from straight diagonal strokes instead of curves — type behaving like cut paper.
Rn Da
Lubalin Graph — 1974
A slab serif drawn with the same geometric discipline as Avant Garde — heavy, confident, unmistakably architectural.
Lg Rt
ITC Serif Gothic — 1974
Drawn with Tony DiSpigna. A serif with none of a serif's usual warmth, sharp wedge-shaped terminals grafted onto the same circular bones as Avant Garde.
Se Go

05 · Close Reading

Anatomy of
a Ligature

Ma&1r23
1 Ligature

The ampersand fuses directly into the neighbouring letters — no counter, no gap, one continuous stroke.

2 Kerning

Letters are pulled far tighter than convention allows, so the word reads as one shape before it reads as text.

3 Weight

A heavy, geometric stroke keeps the mark legible at a glance — logo-scale confidence, not paragraph-scale caution.
